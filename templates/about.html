Data

UCI Banknote Authentication: {{ summary.n_train + summary.n_test }} notes after removing duplicates, with four wavelet-based image features.

2

Models

Logistic Regression, KNN, SVM, Random Forest and a soft-voting ensemble, chosen by 5-fold cross-validated F1.

3

Evaluation

Stratified 80/20 split, confusion matrix, permutation importance and a noise-robustness test.

Two ways to scan

Feature scan uses the benchmark model on four pre-extracted numbers.

Photo scan extracts texture, edge, frequency and colour features from your picture (a Haar wavelet, local binary patterns, FFT bands and HSV statistics) and feeds them to a model trained on real and fake note photos.

Honest limitations

The benchmark dataset is small and very clean, so near-perfect scores are expected. The photo model only knows the currency and camera conditions it was trained on, and phone photos vary a lot. Treat every result as a second opinion, not proof.

Roadmap

A CNN trained on a large photo dataset of local notes, a mobile camera app, explanation of why a note was flagged, and detection of individual security features.
